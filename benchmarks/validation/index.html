Validation Dashboard

The validation dashboard tracks two complementary metrics across releases:

  • Score (0–1) — how closely simulation results match calibration targets from the original BAM model (Delli Gatti et al., 2011). Higher is better.
  • Pass rate (%) — how consistently random seeds produce valid results. A seed “passes” when all its metric scores exceed the tolerance threshold.

Card border colors reflect the score:

  • Green: score ≥ 0.85 — accurate
  • Yellow: score 0.80–0.85 — marginal
  • Red: score < 0.80 — regression

Pass rate is shown inline with its own coloring (green ≥ 97%, yellow ≥ 96%).

Click a scenario card to drill down into per-metric charts. Click any chart to expand it.
